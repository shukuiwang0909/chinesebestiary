## The Case for Zhurong

Zhurong arrives with the full apparatus of a directional god: beast body, human face, two dragons, and the Fire Rectifier's mandate from the *Zuo Zhuan* — the blaze that goes on shining to the four seas. Fire is the phase of summer, of yang at its peak, of the southern sky itself. On the five-phase logic everyone cites, water *should* extinguish fire — but phase logic cuts both ways: a great enough fire boils an ocean dry. Zhurong's lineage is the aristocracy of myth: grandson of Zhuanxu in one telling, and in the *Classic of the Great Wilderness: West*, grandfather of Prince Longqin, whose music moved mountains to dance. Nothing in his file suggests he has ever lost anything.

## The Case for Gonggong

Gonggong's resumé is shorter and far louder. The *Huainanzi* credits him with the single most destructive act in Chinese myth: one head-butt on Mount Buzhou that snapped the pillar of heaven, tilted the sky, and drowned the world's southeast. His minister was the nine-headed Xiangliu; his bloodline produced Houtu, the earth god, and — if the genealogy is read at face value — he descends from the fire god himself, which means he knows every weakness he inherited. A being whose tantrum rewrote the cosmos is not an underdog; he is a natural disaster with a grudge.

## The Verdict

**Zhurong — because the only text that actually stages this fight says so.** Here is the honest evidence trail: the *Shanhaijing* makes them family (father and son, no duel), the *Huainanzi* gives Gonggong a different opponent entirely (Zhuanxu), and the five-phase scheme merely casts them as elemental rivals. It is Sima Zhen's Tang-dynasty supplement to the *Records of the Grand Historian* that finally rings the bell: Gonggong, ruling by force of water, made war on Zhurong — **and lost**, fleeing in his rage to break Mount Buzhou against the sky. The duel verdict is textually Zhurong's. But weigh the consolation prize: the loser walked away owning the most famous broken mountain in the world, and every river in China still runs the way his tantrum left them. **Winner: Zhurong on points — Gonggong on permanent landscape.**
